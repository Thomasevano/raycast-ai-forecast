# AI Forecast review priorities

Trace changed behavior against the linked issue, callers and applicable domain decisions before reporting a defect. A suspicious name or architectural preference is not evidence.

## Failure boundaries

- **Local history:** flag lost samples, reset or plan/profile mixing, cross-account bindings, destructive migrations, swallowed corruption and success reported after a failed write. Opening the view must remain read-only, including absent directories and locks.
- **Quiet alerts:** follow freshness, distinct-reading confidence, sustained-risk gates, exhaustion, retry and durable deduplication. Concurrent monitors must not double-dispatch. Successful dispatch is not proof a banner appeared.
- **External input and privacy:** trace untrusted OpenUsage/public-source data into state, notifications, diagnostics, subprocess arguments and links. Preserve trusted executable selection, bounded reads and separation between public reset hints and personal forecasts. Keep credentials and personal paths out of artifacts.
- **Release safety:** treat persistence, concurrency, migrations and CI changes as high risk. A code revert does not undo history changes or delivered notifications. Build/test success alone does not verify native interactions.

## Signal and convergence

Report actionable behavior, data-integrity or security defects with a narrow location, supported trigger, concrete impact and causal evidence. Distinguish introduced defects, baseline defects and verification gaps; omit style and speculative hardening.

Independently counter-verify candidates. The author fixes; the reviewer reports. Re-review corrections and affected paths only, retain dispositions, and stop after three cycles for a maintainer decision. The maintainer owns merge.
